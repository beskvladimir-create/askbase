What makes it different

The same four questions, two different answers

Typical desktop tool
Askbase
Where the model runs
In the cloud. One vendor states on its own pricing page that running from your device does not mean the processing is offline.
On your disk. The application works with the Wi-Fi switched off.
What protects your database
A read-only role, and instructions in the prompt. Published research shows prompt wording is not a safety measure.
A read-only role, plus a parser. One SELECT only, no recursive CTEs, a row limit and a timeout.
Whether you can check the answer
A table, and usually the SQL.
The table, the SQL, and the assumptions in a plain sentence, including who was left out.
What the accuracy is
Not published on any public benchmark, for any of the eight tools I checked.
Measured and published under a protocol fixed before the run. No number until the run exists.

Askbase is being built in the open. The benchmark, the held-out question set and the protocol will be published; the application itself is in progress. There is no download yet, and this page will carry one when there is.
