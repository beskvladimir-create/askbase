The distinction the table is built around

A desktop application is not the same thing as a local model, and the difference decides whether a regulated organisation may use the tool at all.

AskYourDatabase is the closest product to Askbase in form. It installs on the desktop, connects to PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQL Server, MongoDB and BigQuery, and returns a table, a chart and an explanation. Its Desktop Ultimate seat includes unlimited use of Claude Sonnet 5, Claude Haiku 4.5 and GPT-4.1, which means the model is a cloud service. The vendor states the position plainly on its own pricing page: desktop connections run from your device, and that does not mean the AI processing is offline. A local model is available in the enterprise tier, priced on request, with hosting and maintenance on top.

DataLine reaches privacy from the other direction. It stores everything on the device and, by default, hides the data from the language model it calls. The schema and the question still leave the machine, and the README notes that the hiding can be switched off. It is also licensed GPL-3.0, which is a copyleft licence, and its README currently asks for maintainers.

Asking a question is not the same as sending the question. That is the whole of the distinction, and it is the reason the right-hand column of the table exists.

Two licence facts worth knowing before you build on something

Chat2DB is widely described as open source. For version 5.3.0 and later this is no longer accurate. Its licence is a modified Apache 2.0 with additional conditions: personal use, academic and non-profit self-hosting, and internal use within your own organisation are permitted, while providing an external product or service, managed delivery for an outside party, distributing binaries to an outside party, embedding it in a product, and white-label or OEM delivery each require written authorisation. Releases before 5.3.0 remain under plain Apache 2.0.

WrenAI is multi-licensed by path. The core, SDK, skills and examples are Apache-2.0 and the documentation is CC BY 4.0. The repository also ships the text of AGPL-3.0 in advance, for modules that do not exist yet, and states that the licence table will be updated when they appear. The names and logos are trademarks and are not covered by any of those licences.

The column that is empty for everyone

I could not find a published execution accuracy on a public benchmark, measured under a stated protocol, for any of the eight tools above. Several publish demonstrations. Several are built on models whose authors publish benchmark figures, which is a different claim: a figure for a model is not a figure for the tool wrapped around it.

Askbase does not fill that column either, and will not until the measurement exists. The protocol is fixed in advance, the questions are frozen with a SHA-256 digest before any tool runs on them, and the figures on this site are substituted from the results files by the build, not typed into the page. Until there is a run, the cell reads not yet published.

Why a frozen set, rather than a public benchmark alone. A CIDR 2026 study by Jin, Choi, Zhu and Kang found annotation errors in 52.8% of BIRD Mini-Dev problems and 66.1% of the Spider 2.0-Snow problems with open gold queries. Correcting them moved leading systems by up to 19 points and by as much as three places in the ranking. An absolute score on a public benchmark is therefore not a promise about anyone’s data, including ours.

How to check this page

Every licence and commit date above can be read from the GitHub API in a few seconds, and every price from the vendor’s pricing page. Prices change, so this page carries the date it was checked, and the date is updated when the page is. If you find a figure that has moved, or a tool that belongs here and is missing, write to me and I will correct it.
